SSS Retirement & Survivor Planner

Two retirement decisions that fit the Philippine setting: time your SSS pension (claim at 60 or keep contributing to 65 for a bigger monthly pension), and protect your spouse with the SSS survivorship pension plus an optional private joint-life annuity.

Estimate your SSS monthly pension

The SSS pension depends on your Average Monthly Salary Credit (AMSC) and your Credited Years of Service (CYS) — not on how old you are when you claim. You can take optional retirement at 60 (if separated from work) with no age reduction, or claim at 65. Working longer simply adds CYS, which raises the pension for life.

What your spouse & children receive (SSS survivorship)

Unlike a US joint-and-survivor annuity, SSS survivorship is automatic and free — there is no payout reduction to choose. When a pensioner dies, the legal spouse receives 100% of the basic monthly pension for life (it stops only on remarriage), and dependent children each get a dependent's pension.

Optional: private joint-life annuity (income above SSS)

SSS replaces only part of your income. Many Filipinos add a private annuity (Sun Life, Insular, AXA, Pru Life, etc.), Pag-IBIG MP2 savings, or a personal fund. A private annuity lets you choose how much continues to your spouse — the trade-off the SSS already handles for you.
